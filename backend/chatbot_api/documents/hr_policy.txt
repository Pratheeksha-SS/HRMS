# HR Leave Policy

1. All employees are entitled to 15 Paid Leaves, 12 Sick Leaves, and 10 Casual Leaves per year.
2. Sick leaves can be accumulated up to 30 days. Casual leaves cannot be accumulated.
3. Maternity leave is 6 months. Paternity leave is 2 weeks.
4. If an employee is absent for 3 consecutive days without notice, it is considered absconding.
5. All leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager at least 1 week in advance, except for sick leaves.

# Work Hours
1. Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
2. Employees are expected to work 8 hours a day, 40 hours a week.
3. Lunch break is 1 hour between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
